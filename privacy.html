Privacy & cookies

The short version: I don't track you

Last updated: September 21, 2026

In one paragraph

This is a personal portfolio. It has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no sign-up and no forms. The only way I learn anything about you is if you choose to email me.

Who runs this site

This site is the personal portfolio of Patricia Ladyzhensky, an individual based in Brooklyn, New York, USA. It is not a business and nothing is sold here. For anything on this page, write to ladyzhenskyp@gmail.com.

What this site collects

  • Nothing, by itself. The pages are plain files. There are no accounts, contact forms, newsletter boxes, comment sections, tracking pixels or analytics scripts.
  • Fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact a font provider or any other third party to display these pages.
  • The “Copy email” button places my email address on your clipboard when you press it. It never reads your clipboard, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details (such as browser type and the page requested) when your browser loads a page, and keeps them in logs for security and to operate the service. I do not have access to those logs. GitHub explains its handling of this data in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email me

I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. I use it only to reply and to continue the conversation. My mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), so messages are stored with Google. I do not add you to any list, and I do not sell or share your details. Ask me to delete our correspondence at any time and I will.

Cookie policy

This site sets no cookies and does not use local storage, session storage or any similar technology on your device. Because nothing is stored or read on your device, there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

If that ever changes (for example, if I add visitor statistics) I will update this page first, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Links and demo apps

This site links to other sites, including LinkedIn, GitHub, and my project demos (the onboarding tracker, Stan's Pitch and focus · flow). Those are separate sites that run on other services and have their own practices; this page does not cover them.

  • The demos exist to show how something works. Please do not enter real personal, client or health information into any demo.
  • The onboarding tracker demo contains only fictional companies and people, uses one cookie that keeps you signed in, and erases all changes every hour. Anything a visitor adds there is visible to other visitors until that reset. It loads its typeface from Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry), which therefore receives your IP address; everything else is served by the demo itself.

Children

This site is meant for recruiters, hiring managers and colleagues. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information someone holds about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. The only information I could hold is an email you sent me. Write to ladyzhenskyp@gmail.com and I will answer, whatever your location.

I do not sell or share personal information. The site treats every visitor the same whether or not their browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal, because it does not track anyone in the first place.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
